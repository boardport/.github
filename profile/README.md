### $ cat /usr/share/boardport/mission.txt

**BoardPort** is a research ecosystem and open laboratory dedicated to giving hardware a second life by transforming vendor-locked devices into
autonomous Linux nodes. Driven entirely by community enthusiasm, the project exists as an independent open-source initiative, free from commercial
constraints and corporate agendas.

Every year, millions of capable ARM/ARM64 single-board computers, 4G LTE modems, routers, and mobile devices are decommissioned solely due to
abandoned vendor software and outdated proprietary kernels. We are changing this paradigm: instead of disposal, we offer deep hardware
revitalization—unlocking its true potential and expanding its operational horizons.

**Core Focus Areas:**

* 🛠️ **Low-Level Regeneration:** Building containerized tooling for firmware dumping, safe unbricking/recovery (EDL/BootROM), and hardware reverse
  engineering.
* 🚀 **Mainline & Open Bootloaders:** Porting open secondary bootloaders (`lk2nd`, `U-Boot`) and adapting fresh vanilla Linux kernels stripped of
  vendor bloat.
* 🌐 **Edge Infrastructure:** Repurposing forgotten hardware into predictable, energy-efficient micro-servers, network gateways, edge routers, and
  automation nodes powered by Alpine, OpenWrt, and Debian.

We prove that "e-waste" is merely a symptom of missing software, turning discarded electronics into a solid foundation for distributed infrastructure.
